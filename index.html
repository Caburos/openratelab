COMMON QUESTIONS

What's included in the free email audit? [+]

We review your Klaviyo account: flow architecture, active campaigns, list health, and deliverability signals. You get a written breakdown of what's working, what's not, and what we'd prioritize fixing first. No pitch attached.

How long until we see results from Klaviyo flows? [+]

Flow rebuilds typically show measurable impact within the first 30 days, once the welcome sequence and abandonment flows are live. Full system performance compounds over 60–90 days as data accumulates and segments sharpen.

Do you work with brands on Shopify or WooCommerce? [+]

Yes. We work primarily with Shopify brands, since Klaviyo's Shopify integration is where we're most effective. We also work with WooCommerce accounts where Klaviyo is already the ESP.

What's a good open rate for Klaviyo emails? [+]

Across our active accounts, flows average 40–55%+ and campaigns 35–50%+. Industry averages run 25–30%, but those include broken setups and cold lists. If you're below 30% on flows, something fundamental is wrong with list health, subject line strategy, or both.

How does your Klaviyo email management work? [+]

We scope each engagement after the audit. Account size, send volume, and whether you need full management or copy-only support all affect scope. The audit is always the right starting point. Book one and we'll give you a straight answer on what your account needs.
