## The Problem

SendGrid earns its reputation on transactional and API-based email delivery, order confirmations, password resets, receipts. When brands also run marketing campaigns through it, the marketing side often gets far less attention than the deliverability infrastructure it's built on, and campaigns end up under-segmented and inconsistently sent.

## What We Do on SendGrid

- **Deliverability and authentication work**, SPF, DKIM, and DMARC configured correctly, since this is SendGrid's core strength and the foundation everything else depends on.
- **Marketing Campaigns management**, for brands using SendGrid's marketing product: segmentation, content, and a consistent sending calendar.
- **Separation of transactional and marketing sending**, so promotional volume doesn't put transactional deliverability (order confirmations, receipts) at risk.
- **An honest read on platform fit**, since a dedicated marketing ESP is often the better long-term home for campaigns once volume and segmentation needs grow.

## Who It's For

Brands using SendGrid's Marketing Campaigns product who want it run with real segmentation and cadence, or brands who need SendGrid's deliverability infrastructure managed properly alongside a separate marketing platform.
