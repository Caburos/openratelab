## The Problem

MailerLite's simplicity is its main selling point, and also the reason a lot of accounts stay underbuilt. Because the platform is easy to use, brands often set up one automation and stop there, rather than building out the full lifecycle the platform is actually capable of running.

## What We Do on MailerLite

- **Automation workflows** built around your actual customer lifecycle: welcome, abandoned cart, and post-purchase sequences, not just the one default automation most accounts start with.
- **Segmentation** based on purchase history and engagement, so campaigns target the right subset of your list.
- **Campaign planning and copy**, run to a consistent calendar.
- **An honest read on platform fit**, since MailerLite is a genuinely good choice for smaller lists and simpler catalogs, and we'll say so rather than pushing a migration you don't need.

## Who It's For

Smaller ecommerce and DTC brands on MailerLite who want the platform actually built out, or brands wondering whether they've outgrown it. A free audit gives you a straight answer either way, alongside our full [service list](/services/) if a deeper build ends up being the right call.
