## The Problem

Omnisend is a genuinely ecommerce-focused platform, but plenty of accounts never get past the default workflow templates it ships with. Segmentation stays generic, campaigns go out inconsistently, and the platform's native Shopify data never gets used to actually target anyone specifically.

## What We Do on Omnisend

- **Workflow automation**, Omnisend's equivalent of lifecycle flows, built around abandoned cart, welcome, browse abandonment, and post-purchase behavior.
- **Segmentation using native store data**, since Omnisend's Shopify integration surfaces purchase history and product data that too many accounts never actually segment against.
- **Campaign planning and copy**, run to a calendar instead of sent inconsistently.
- **Coordination with SMS**, where it's already part of the account, so email and SMS don't compete for the same customer's attention.

## Who It's For

Ecommerce brands already on Omnisend who want its native ecommerce features actually built out, not just left on defaults.
