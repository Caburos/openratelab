## The Problem

A lot of Mailchimp accounts run on whatever was set up once, years ago: a default welcome email, no real automation, campaigns sent whenever someone remembers. Mailchimp is a capable platform, but capability doesn't build itself. The gap is usually that nobody's actually built out the system it's capable of running.

## What We Do on Mailchimp

- **Campaign planning and copy**, run to a real calendar instead of sent whenever someone has time.
- **Customer Journeys automation**, Mailchimp's workflow builder, configured around your actual customer lifecycle rather than left on defaults.
- **Audience segmentation**, built around purchase behavior and engagement rather than one undivided list.
- **Deliverability monitoring**, since authentication and list hygiene matter on every platform, not just Klaviyo.

## Who It's For

Brands already on Mailchimp who aren't ready to migrate platforms, or brands that want Mailchimp actually built out properly before deciding whether a move to a more ecommerce-specific platform makes sense.
