## The Problem

Health and wellness brands are often stuck on the wrong foundation: a general-purpose ESP, a list that's never been cleaned, no automation covering the customer lifecycle. On top of that, the category comes with real constraints on what can be claimed, which means copy has to build trust through education and consistency rather than promotional pressure.

## How We Approach Health & Wellness Email

- **Full account rebuilds when the foundation is wrong**, platform migration, list hygiene, and flow architecture handled in the right order, rather than patching a broken setup piece by piece.
- **Automation-first flow architecture**, since wellness purchases tend to be recurring and habit-driven, making lifecycle flows the highest-leverage investment.
- **Education-led, trust-building copy**, grounded in what the brand can actually substantiate rather than aggressive claims.
- **Consistent, planned sending** in place of sporadic campaigns, which matters for a category where trust builds over repeated, reliable contact.

## Proof From Health & Wellness Accounts

One lifestyle and wellness brand was on the wrong platform with no automation and a list that had never been cleaned. A full migration and rebuild, in the right order, took it to a 75.8% average open rate and $52,724 in revenue across 12 months. Another nutrition brand, previously treated as an afterthought with no flows and inconsistent sends, moved to an automation-first system and generated $64,838 in 12 months.
