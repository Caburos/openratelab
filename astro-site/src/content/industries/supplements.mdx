## The Problem

Most supplement brands treat every email like a first-sale email: a discount, a product feature, a generic send. But a supplement business runs on reorders, and reorder timing is predictable. A customer who bought a 30-day supply is going to run out in roughly 30 days, and if the reminder doesn't land before then, that customer churns to whatever's easiest to reorder, sometimes a competitor, sometimes nothing at all.

## How We Approach Supplement Email

- **Replenishment flows timed to actual product usage**, not a generic 30-60-90 day cadence applied to every product regardless of dosage or supply size.
- **Education-led campaign content** that builds trust in the product's mechanism and use case, which matters more in a category where customers are evaluating whether something actually works for them.
- **Segmentation by subscriber status and purchase history**, so a first-time buyer and a 12-month subscriber get meaningfully different messaging instead of the same blast.
- **Win-back sequences** built for customers who lapsed past their reorder window, since a lapsed supplement customer is often still interested, just not thinking about it until reminded.

## Proof From Supplement Accounts

One account running the full system: 191 campaigns and 193 active flows across 2.4 million email delivers, holding a 51.5% average open rate the entire time. Another went from a passive list with no system at all to email becoming the brand's number one revenue channel, generating $172K in 12 months once campaigns, automations, and segmentation were actually built.
