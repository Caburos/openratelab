## The Problem

Fashion and activewear brands often run email reactively: a new collection drops, someone scrambles to write and send a campaign that week, and the cycle repeats with no real system behind it. That approach burns through discount codes to compensate for a list that isn't segmented and content that isn't planned, which erodes margin and, over time, deliverability.

## How We Approach Fashion & Activewear Email

- **A planned campaign calendar** built around your actual drop schedule, with copy and segmentation ready before launch day, not written the morning of.
- **Segmentation by product category and purchase history**, so engaged buyers of a specific line see relevant new arrivals first, instead of every customer getting every campaign.
- **Deliverability protected through relevance**, not volume reduction: sending the right content to the right segment keeps unsubscribe and spam-complaint rates low even at a high send frequency.
- **Flows that don't rely on discounting**: abandoned cart, browse abandonment, and post-purchase sequences that use product storytelling and urgency signals other than price cuts.

## Proof From Fashion & Activewear Accounts

One activewear account held a 40.3% open rate and a 2.66% click rate, both above industry average, simultaneously, across 58 flows and 46 campaigns, with an unsubscribe rate under 0.32%. That's what a fast sending cadence looks like when it's built on segmentation and relevance instead of just volume.
