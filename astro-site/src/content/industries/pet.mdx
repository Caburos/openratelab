## The Problem

Pet brands often default to the same promotional playbook as any other ecommerce category: discount codes, generic product feature emails, seasonal blasts. That undersells the category's real advantage. Pet owners are unusually engaged with content about their animals, and email that leans into that (education, story, curiosity) tends to outperform email that leans on price.

## How We Approach Pet Brand Email

- **Story-led and curiosity-driven copy**, prioritized over discount-first messaging, since pet owners respond to content that speaks to their relationship with the animal, not just the transaction.
- **Consumption-based replenishment flows**, timed to how quickly a specific product (food, treats, supplements) actually gets used up, not a flat generic timeline.
- **Segmentation by pet type and product category** where the catalog spans multiple species or use cases, so a dog owner isn't getting cat-specific content and vice versa.
- **Non-promotional subject lines** tested against curiosity and education angles, which have held sustained engagement over multi-year periods in accounts we manage.

## Proof From Pet Accounts

One account held a 72-76% open rate for over two years using curiosity-driven, non-promotional subject lines, not a spike, a sustained pattern. Another generated $33,454 from a single story-led, no-discount campaign, more than most brands see from a full month of promotions. A third produced the best revenue-per-recipient in our current portfolio ($0.75) from a list under 1,200 people, with zero discount incentive.
