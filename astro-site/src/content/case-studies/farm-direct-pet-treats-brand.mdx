## The Objective

This farm-direct pet treats brand had a small, highly engaged list of
roughly 1,000 subscribers. Small lists get written off fast in this
industry. People assume there isn't enough volume to matter.

The brand wanted proof that email could still be a serious revenue
channel without the scale a bigger brand has. We took that as the actual
test.

**List size:** ~1,000 &nbsp;&nbsp; **Discount incentive:** NONE

## What We Did

Instead of chasing volume, we built one send around identity. No coupon
code to hide behind if it didn't work.

We built an identity-framed seasonal send, timed to a real cultural
moment, with no discount code attached. The copy leaned entirely on
emotional connection to the product's origin story rather than price.

The best per-recipient efficiency of any account currently in our
portfolio review. Flagged internally as an early, promising pattern we're
continuing to validate: **$0.75** revenue per recipient, no incentive.

## The Delta

Quantitative outcomes from the account's Klaviyo campaign reporting. The
send produced $0.75 in revenue per recipient with zero discount. On a
list this size, that's the strongest per-recipient number we've recorded
across any account.

- **Revenue per recipient:** $0.75 (no incentive)
- **List size:** ~1,000 (small & engaged)
