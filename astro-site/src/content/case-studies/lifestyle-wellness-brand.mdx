## The Objective

The brand was on the wrong email platform with no automation
infrastructure. The list had never been cleaned. Starting over was the
only viable path: patching the existing setup would have locked in the
problems.

We migrated to Klaviyo, cleaned the list before sending a single email,
and built a complete flow architecture alongside a consistent campaign
calendar.

**Campaigns sent:** 94 &nbsp;&nbsp; **Flows built:** 42

## What We Did

A full teardown and rebuild. Platform migration, list hygiene, flow
architecture, and campaign system, in that order.

We migrated the full account to Klaviyo and rebuilt the account structure
before importing a single contact. Clean architecture from day one, not
retrofitted onto a broken foundation.

Deep list clean before the first campaign. Suppression rules set.
Unengaged removed.

Welcome to win-back, and every lifecycle stage in between. 42 flows
covering the complete customer journey, built to run without manual input
and optimized over time.

$31,295 campaigns + $21,428 flows across 12 months of managed sending:
**$53K** total revenue.

## The Delta

Quantitative outcomes from the account's Klaviyo reporting across 12
months. 75.8% average open rate on a brand that was previously not
tracking opens at all. Platform migration done right: not just a tool
switch, a full system rebuild.

- **Avg open rate:** 75.8% (vs 25% industry)
- **Total email revenue:** $52,724 (12 months)
- **Campaign revenue:** $31,295 (94 sends)
- **Flow revenue:** $21,428 (42 flows)
