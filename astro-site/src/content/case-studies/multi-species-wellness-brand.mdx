## The Objective

This multi-species animal wellness brand had a healthy list and a real
budget behind it. Most of its email revenue came from discount-driven
promos.

That pattern trains subscribers to wait for the next sale instead of
buying the moment an email lands. Every campaign was competing against the
memory of the last discount.

**Program length:** 7 MO &nbsp;&nbsp; **Typical monthly revenue:** $84K–$140K

## What We Did

We wanted to prove the list would respond to something other than a
percentage off. So we built a send with no discount code attached at all.

We wrote a single campaign built entirely around brand narrative. No
discount code, no urgency gimmick. The send leaned on a mission-linked
angle the brand cared about, framed as a reason to buy today
instead of waiting for a deal.

Two separate no-incentive, narrative-led campaigns, both outperforming the
account's typical promotional send:

- **Story send #1:** $33,454
- **Story send #2:** $21,060

## The Delta

Quantitative outcomes from the account's Klaviyo campaign reporting. The
send closed at $33,454 with zero markdown. A second story-led send later
produced $21,060, also with no incentive. Neither was a fluke.

- **Revenue per recipient:** $2.01 (top campaign)
- **Open rate:** 59% (no-incentive send)
- **Second story send:** $21,060 (also no incentive)
