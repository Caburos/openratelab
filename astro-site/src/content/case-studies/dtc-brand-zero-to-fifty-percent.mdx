## The Objective

This account had no functioning email program. In the 30 days before the engagement began, email-attributed revenue was $0.00, precisely 0.00% of total store revenue. Every sale was coming from somewhere else entirely.

**State before:** No email program (0.00% attributed) &nbsp;&nbsp; **Status:** Ongoing

## What We Did

We built the account from the ground up: list capture, core flows, and a first campaign, in that order, so there was something for a subscriber to receive from day one rather than a list sitting unused.

<div class="screenshot-proof">
  <img src="/images/case-studies/client2-firstcampaign.png" alt="Klaviyo business performance summary showing 22.14% attributed revenue after the first campaign" loading="lazy" />
  <span class="screenshot-caption">[ CLIENT DASHBOARD // FIRST CAMPAIGN SENT: 22.14% ATTRIBUTED ]</span>
</div>

A single campaign, the first email this list had ever received, generated 22.14% of total store revenue for that period on its own.

## The Delta

Quantitative outcomes from the account's own Klaviyo reporting.

<div class="screenshot-proof">
  <img src="/images/case-studies/client2-month1.png" alt="Klaviyo business performance summary showing 50.62% attributed revenue in the first full month" loading="lazy" />
  <span class="screenshot-caption">[ CLIENT DASHBOARD // MONTH 1: 50.62% ATTRIBUTED ]</span>
</div>

Within the first full month of a real program running, email accounted for 50.62% of total store revenue, up from a standing start of exactly $0.

<div class="screenshot-proof">
  <img src="/images/case-studies/client2-month2.png" alt="Klaviyo business performance summary showing 33.70% attributed revenue in the second month" loading="lazy" />
  <span class="screenshot-caption">[ CLIENT DASHBOARD // MONTH 2: 33.70% ATTRIBUTED ]</span>
</div>

The second month held at 33.70% of total revenue attributed to email, even as total store revenue itself dropped for the period, a normal seasonal swing rather than a sign the email program had stalled.

- **Before:** $0.00 attributed (0.00% of revenue)
- **First campaign:** $2,176.97 attributed (22.14% of revenue)
- **Month 1:** $6,689.33 attributed (50.62% of revenue)
- **Month 2:** $2,580.42 attributed (33.70% of revenue)
