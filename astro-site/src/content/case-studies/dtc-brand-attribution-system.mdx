## The Objective

This DTC brand treated email as an afterthought. An occasional newsletter
went out when someone remembered to send it. No flows ran in the
background. No campaign calendar existed.

Deliverability was never managed, so even the sends that did go out
underperformed. Email wasn't a channel the founder could rely on. It was
a task that got skipped most weeks.

**State before:** Sporadic Sends &nbsp;&nbsp; **Status:** Ongoing

## What We Did

We treated email as infrastructure, not a task list item. That meant
building the parts that run without anyone remembering to hit send.

We built a proper email system: automated flows, a real campaign
calendar, and a deliverability strategy to keep the account healthy.
Occasional sends were replaced with a system that runs on its own.

Consistent, ongoing email revenue attribution since the system replaced
sporadic, occasional sends: **30%+** email revenue attribution, ongoing.

## The Delta

Quantitative outcomes from the account's Klaviyo reporting. Email revenue
attribution has held consistently above 30% since the system went live.
The founder no longer has to think about whether an email went out this
week.

- **Email attribution:** 30%+ (ongoing)
- **State before:** Sporadic Sends (no system)
- **Status:** Ongoing (active)
