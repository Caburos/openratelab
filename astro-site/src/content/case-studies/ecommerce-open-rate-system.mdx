## The Objective

This e-commerce brand had built a sizable list, over 15,000 active
subscribers, but the account behind it was not built to convert. Flows
were generic. Campaigns went out on no real schedule. Segmentation barely
existed.

The list size should have supported strong, consistent revenue. It
wasn't. Open rates sat where any unmanaged account sits, and campaign
revenue moved up and down with no clear pattern.

**Active list:** 15,000+ &nbsp;&nbsp; **Campaign revenue:** $123K / MO

## What We Did

We didn't add more emails. We rebuilt the system underneath them, so
every send had a reason to exist and a segment it was actually written
for.

We rebuilt the account's core flows and campaign calendar from the ground
up. Copy, segmentation, and send cadence were all engineered specifically
for a 15,000+ subscriber list, not a generic template stretched to fit
it.

Consistent monthly campaign revenue on a rebuilt flow and campaign
system, sustained month over month: **$123K / MO** — sustained campaign
revenue.

## The Delta

Quantitative outcomes from the account's Klaviyo reporting. Open rate
climbed to 70.7% and held there. Campaign revenue settled into a steady
$123K a month, on the same list that used to produce inconsistent
results.

- **Avg open rate:** 70.7% (sustained)
- **Active list:** 15,000+ subscribers
- **Campaign revenue:** $123K / MO (ongoing)
