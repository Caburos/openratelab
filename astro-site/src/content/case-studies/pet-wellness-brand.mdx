## The Objective

This DTC pet wellness brand had years of send history but no consistent
formula for what got opened. Open rates swung depending on who
wrote the subject line that week.

Some sends did well. Others barely registered. There was no repeatable
system behind the good weeks, just guesswork that occasionally landed.

**Program length:** 2+ YRS &nbsp;&nbsp; **Validated repeats:** 4+

## What We Did

We stopped guessing and started testing one variable at a time, until a
clear, repeatable pattern showed up in the data.

We standardized on curiosity-driven, non-promotional subject lines
instead of generic sale language. We paired them with holiday-tied
campaigns offering a consistent incentive, tested and repeated across
more than four separate occasions.

Consistent revenue on the same campaign format, replicated across 4+
occasions (a validated, repeatable pattern, not a one-off spike):
**$698–$802** revenue per holiday-tied send.

## The Delta

Quantitative outcomes from the account's Klaviyo campaign reporting. The
open rate has held between 72% and 76% for over two years running. The
holiday-tied campaign format alone has produced $698 to $802 every time
it runs.

- **Open rate range:** 72-76% (sustained)
- **Campaign revenue:** $698–$802 (holiday format)
- **Program history:** 2+ YRS (consistent)
