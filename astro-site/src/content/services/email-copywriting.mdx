## The Problem

A lot of email copy reads like it was written for any brand. It could be swapped between two unrelated stores and nobody would notice. That's the tell of copy written from a template instead of from the account's actual data: what this specific list responds to, where in the customer journey this specific email lands, and what this specific subscriber needs to hear right now.

## What's Included

- **Flow copy:** welcome series, abandoned cart, browse abandonment, post-purchase, win-back, and any lifecycle stage specific to your business.
- **Campaign copy:** launches, promotions, editorial and story-led sends, written to a calendar instead of produced last-minute.
- **Subject line and preview text testing:** the first two things a subscriber sees, treated as seriously as the body copy.
- **Voice work grounded in your brand**, not a generic direct-response template. See our [about page](/about/) for how we think about brand voice versus data.

## How It Works

Copy gets written after we've looked at what the account's data shows: open rate by segment, click behavior, and what's already converting versus what's dead weight. That's the difference between copy that sounds good and copy that performs, and it's why results like a single campaign outperforming a full month of typical promotional sends are achievable without discounting.

## Who It's For

Brands that already have flows and segmentation in place but whose copy isn't converting, or brands starting a full build who want copy handled by people who write to the data, not to a template. If the gap is upstream, no flows or account structure at all, start with [Klaviyo setup](/services/klaviyo-setup/).
