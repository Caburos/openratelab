## The Problem

A list with no segmentation gets treated as one audience: everyone gets the same campaign, the same offer, the same cadence. That means your best customers get generic treatment, and your least engaged subscribers get sent to just as often, quietly damaging deliverability with every send that gets ignored or marked as spam.

## What's Included

- **Behavioral segment architecture:** built around purchase history, engagement level, and product affinity specific to your catalog.
- **Dynamic (not static) segmentation:** segments that update automatically as customer behavior changes, so the work doesn't need to be redone every quarter.
- **Segment-specific campaign and flow targeting:** the right message to the right subset of your list, instead of one send to everyone.
- **Ongoing review:** segmentation logic reassessed as your list and business change, not set once during onboarding and forgotten.

## How It Works

Segmentation work starts with understanding what actually distinguishes your customers: high-value repeat buyers versus one-time purchasers, engaged subscribers versus dormant ones, and any product-line-specific behavior worth targeting separately. That structure then feeds every flow and campaign, so targeting improves without rebuilding the rest of the account.

## Who It's For

Any account sending to one undivided list, or an account with segments that were built once during onboarding and never revisited as the list grew. Segmentation is a component of most engagements rather than a standalone fix; see [Klaviyo Flows & Automation](/services/klaviyo-flows-automation/) if the underlying flows themselves also need work.
