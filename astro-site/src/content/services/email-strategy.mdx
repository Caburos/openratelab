## The Problem

Most email "strategy" is a generic 90-day content calendar template with your logo swapped in. It doesn't account for your list size, your catalog, your purchase cycle, or what's already been tried. A plan that isn't built on your account's actual data is a guess with a nice template around it.

## What's Included

- **Full account audit as the starting point:** list health, flow coverage, segmentation, and campaign history, reviewed before any plan gets written.
- **A prioritized 90-day roadmap:** sequenced by revenue impact: what to fix first, what to build next, and why, tied to numbers from your own account.
- **Realistic targets**, not invented industry benchmarks. Every target is grounded in what's achievable given your list size, catalog, and current baseline.
- **A plan you keep**, whether you implement it in-house, with an existing team, or ask us to execute it.

## How It Works

Strategy work is the "roadmap" step of the four-step approach behind every OpenRateLab engagement: audit, roadmap, build, compound. We don't skip the audit to get to a plan faster, since a plan built on assumptions instead of your actual data usually targets the wrong problem.

## Who It's For

Brands that want a clear, sequenced plan before committing to a full build, or brands with an in-house team who want an outside, data-grounded roadmap to execute against. If you already know what needs fixing and just need it built, [Klaviyo Flows & Automation](/services/klaviyo-flows-automation/) or [Klaviyo management](/services/klaviyo-management/) may be the more direct fit.
