## The Problem

Email marketing often gets treated as a task instead of a channel: a promotion goes out when there's a sale, a newsletter goes out when someone has time. Nobody's planning three sends ahead, nobody's tracking which campaigns actually drive revenue versus which ones just drive unsubscribes, and the account never builds the kind of consistency that compounds.

## What's Included

- **Campaign planning:** a calendar built around your actual launches, promotions, and content, planned weeks ahead instead of decided the morning of.
- **Copywriting and design coordination:** every send written and built to convert, not just to fill a calendar slot.
- **List segmentation:** sends targeted to the right subset of your list based on behavior and purchase history, not blasted to everyone every time.
- **Reporting tied to revenue:** monthly numbers on what each campaign generated, so decisions get made on data instead of gut feel.

## How It Works

We start by mapping your current sending pattern against what's actually converting, then build a calendar and segmentation structure around what the data shows. From there it's ongoing execution: plan, write, send, report, adjust. The same four-step approach (audit, roadmap, build, compound) that runs across every engagement at OpenRateLab.

Ecommerce brands sending email inconsistently, or brands with a Klaviyo account that isn't being used to its full potential for planned, revenue-driving campaigns.
