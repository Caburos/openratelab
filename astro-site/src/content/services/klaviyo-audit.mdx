## The Problem

Most brands have a vague sense that email "could be doing better" without knowing specifically why. Is it the flows? The list? Deliverability? Without a specific answer, the fix ends up being generic, an agency that promises to "improve engagement" without pointing to what's actually broken.

## What's Included

- **Flow architecture review:** which lifecycle stages are covered, which are missing, and where existing flows break down.
- **Campaign history analysis:** what's converting, what isn't, and why, based on your account's actual send history.
- **List health check:** segmentation quality, list hygiene, and whether your list is being maintained or just growing unchecked.
- **Deliverability signals:** sender reputation, unsubscribe patterns, and inbox placement risk factors.
- **A written, prioritized roadmap:** specific fixes ranked by revenue impact, not a generic 90-day template applied to every account.

## How It Works

This is the first of the four steps behind every engagement at OpenRateLab: audit, roadmap, build, compound. The audit is where we look at your actual account instead of assuming what's wrong. A rebuild based on a real audit is what took one account from average performance to a sustained 70.7% open rate, not a guess applied at random.

Any ecommerce or DTC brand on Klaviyo (or considering it) who wants a specific, honest read of their account before deciding what to fix.
