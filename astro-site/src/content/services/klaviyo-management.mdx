## The Problem

Most Klaviyo accounts are half-managed. A welcome flow was built once and never touched again. Campaigns go out when someone remembers. Segmentation is either missing or built once and never revisited as the list changes. Nobody owns the account as a whole, so small gaps compound into a channel that underperforms every month, quietly.

## What's Included

Klaviyo management means one team is accountable for the entire account, not just one piece of it:

- **Flow maintenance and builds:** welcome, abandoned cart, browse abandonment, post-purchase, win-back, and any lifecycle stage specific to your business, built once and kept current as your offers and catalog change.
- **Campaign calendar:** a consistent, planned send schedule instead of sporadic sends, with copy and design handled per campaign.
- **Segmentation:** lists that reflect actual customer behavior (engagement, purchase history, product affinity), rebuilt as the account grows instead of left static.
- **Deliverability monitoring:** list hygiene, suppression rules, and sender reputation tracked on an ongoing basis, not just fixed once during onboarding.
- **Monthly reporting:** open rate, click rate, and revenue attribution reported against your account's own baseline, not generic industry benchmarks.

## How It Works

Every management engagement starts with the same four steps we use across the agency: audit the current account, build a prioritized roadmap, execute the build, then run the account month over month so results compound instead of resetting. See the full breakdown on the [Klaviyo audit](/services/klaviyo-audit/) page if you want to understand what that first step looks like in detail.

## Who It's For

Brands already on Klaviyo, sending at least some email, who need the account run properly instead of maintained in pieces. If you don't have a Klaviyo account yet, start with [Klaviyo setup](/services/klaviyo-setup/) instead.
