## The Problem

Some brands are running entirely without email infrastructure: no Klaviyo account, no flows, no list. Every sale is a one-time transaction with no system capturing the customer for a second one. Starting from zero is actually an advantage: there's no legacy setup to work around, everything gets built correctly from the first line of code.

## What's Included

- **Account structure and integration:** Klaviyo connected to your store platform correctly, with custom event tracking, from day one.
- **Core flow architecture:** welcome, abandoned cart, browse abandonment, post-purchase, and win-back, built to cover the full customer lifecycle.
- **List segmentation from the start:** behavioral segments built into the account structure, not bolted on later.
- **Campaign calendar:** a real sending cadence in place before the account goes live, so it's earning from week one, not sitting idle while a plan gets figured out.

## How It Works

Setup starts the same way every engagement does: understanding your catalog, purchase cycle, and customer base, so the account structure fits your business instead of a generic default. From there it's sequenced so the highest-impact pieces (welcome and abandoned cart) go live first, with the fuller build following as data accumulates.

## Who It's For

Brands with no Klaviyo account yet, or an account that was created but never actually configured. If you already have a working account and just need it run better, [Klaviyo management](/services/klaviyo-management/) or a [Klaviyo audit](/services/klaviyo-audit/) is the more precise starting point.
