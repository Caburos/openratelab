## The Problem

Most Klaviyo accounts have a welcome flow, built once during onboarding, and nothing else. Abandoned cart is either missing or running on default settings nobody's touched since setup. Post-purchase and win-back don't exist at all. That's not automation, that's one flow doing all the work while the rest of the customer lifecycle goes unaddressed.

## What's Included

- **Full lifecycle flow architecture:** welcome, abandoned cart, browse abandonment, post-purchase, win-back, and any custom flow specific to your business model.
- **Custom event tracking:** flows triggered by behavior specific to your store (not just Klaviyo's defaults), so automation reflects how your customers actually shop.
- **Dynamic segmentation feeding the flows:** lists that update automatically as customer behavior changes, so flows stay targeted without manual upkeep.
- **Testing and optimization:** flows aren't built once and left; timing, copy, and triggers get reviewed as performance data comes in.

## How It Works

Flow work starts with mapping the actual customer journey for your business: what happens between someone landing on the site and someone becoming a repeat customer, and where the gaps are. From there we build the flows that cover those gaps, in priority order, so revenue impact shows up as each piece goes live rather than waiting for a full rebuild to finish. This is the same audit-first approach behind every engagement, detailed on the [Klaviyo audit](/services/klaviyo-audit/) page.

## Who It's For

Brands with a Klaviyo account and some flows already live, whose automation stops at the welcome series, or whose flows were built once years ago and never revisited. If there's no Klaviyo account yet, [Klaviyo setup](/services/klaviyo-setup/) is the starting point instead.
