## Outline
- Why "which platform is best" is the wrong question (every platform has
  real limits at scale; the useful question is what to do about them)
- Limitation 1: segmentation performance on large lists (slow/complex
  segment builds) — workaround: pre-computed tags/flags via flows instead
  of live segment conditions for the heaviest filters
- Limitation 2: cross-channel attribution overlap (email + SMS both
  claiming credit for the same conversion) — workaround: a clear
  attribution-window/priority rule, not trusting either channel's
  dashboard in isolation
- Limitation 3: template editor limits for complex dynamic content —
  workaround: modular blocks built once, reused via saved sections rather
  than rebuilding per campaign
- Limitation 4: deliverability visibility (inbox placement isn't directly
  shown) — workaround: seed-list monitoring and engagement-tier tracking
  as a proxy
- The real lesson: platform limitations are a scoping problem, not a
  reason to switch tools
