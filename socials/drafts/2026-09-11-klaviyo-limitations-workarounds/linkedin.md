Every platform has limits. The agencies that look like they don't are just better at working around them.

Four we actually run into on Klaviyo accounts, and how we handle each:

1. Segmentation gets slow on large lists with complex live conditions. Fix: pre-compute a tag via a flow instead of evaluating the condition live every send.

2. Email and SMS both claim credit for the same conversion. Fix: a fixed attribution window and priority rule, applied consistently, not trusting either dashboard's raw number.

3. The template editor hits a wall on complex dynamic content. Fix: build modular saved sections once, reuse them, instead of rebuilding custom blocks per campaign.

4. No direct inbox-placement visibility. Fix: engagement-tier tracking and seed-list testing as a proxy signal.

None of these are reasons to switch platforms. They're known friction points with known fixes.
