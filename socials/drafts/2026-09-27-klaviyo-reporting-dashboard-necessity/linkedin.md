Someone just asked Reddit if they should sell their custom Klaviyo reporting dashboard as a product. Before answering that, here's the question that actually matters first.

"Is native reporting enough" isn't answerable in the abstract. It depends on which specific view is missing. Most requests for "better reporting" turn out to be one specific cross-reference Klaviyo doesn't surface in a single screen, not a real gap in the data.

Klaviyo's native reporting already covers flow performance, campaign performance, segments, and revenue attribution well. The two genuine gaps we see are lifecycle-stage revenue (welcome vs. repeat vs. win-back) and multi-store rollups when someone runs several accounts.

Before building or buying anything: name the exact number, broken down the exact way, that requires manual work every time someone asks. If that gap is structural, tooling earns its cost. If it's a five-minute report someone forgot to check, that's the actual fix.
