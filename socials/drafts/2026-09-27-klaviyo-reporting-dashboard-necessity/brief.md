## Outline

- The actual question isn't "is native reporting enough" — it's which specific view is missing
- What Klaviyo's built-in reporting already covers well (flow performance, campaign performance, segment membership, revenue attribution)
- Where the real gaps usually are: cross-flow moment coverage, revenue per customer lifecycle stage, multi-store or multi-brand rollups
- When a separate dashboard is genuinely justified vs. when it's solving a diagnosis problem with a tooling purchase
