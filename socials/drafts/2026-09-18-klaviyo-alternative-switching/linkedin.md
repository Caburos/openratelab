"Klaviyo alternative" gets searched constantly. Most of the time, the platform isn't the actual problem. Here's what genuinely justifies switching, and what's almost always solving the wrong thing.

Three things usually drive the search: cost at scale, a specific limitation someone just hit, or general frustration after a rough month. Only the first is actually a platform problem. The other two are account problems wearing a platform's name.

What justifies switching: a genuine structural mismatch, pricing that scales badly for your volume, an integration requirement the platform doesn't support, a catalog structure it can't represent cleanly. Built into how the platform works, not how it's configured.

What doesn't: a limitation with a known workaround, a bad month, or an account that was never set up correctly. Switching doesn't fix a poorly configured account. It recreates the same problems on new infrastructure.

Migration costs real time too: flow history doesn't transfer, integrations get reconnected from scratch, months of degraded performance while flows rebuild. "Just switch" advice almost never factors that in.
