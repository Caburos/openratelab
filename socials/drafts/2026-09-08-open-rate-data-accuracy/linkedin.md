Your open rate didn't get better. Apple started pre-opening your emails for privacy reasons before a human ever saw them. Here's what that actually changes, and what it doesn't.

iOS Mail Privacy Protection pre-loads the tracking pixel in any email as soon as it arrives, not when someone actually opens it. That counts as an "open" in your reporting whether or not a human ever looked. It affects a real share of most consumer lists.

What it distorts: open rate, for the Apple Mail portion of your list.

What it doesn't touch: click rate, and revenue. Both still require an actual action.

So the fix isn't "stop trusting your data." It's knowing which numbers are still reliable (clicks, revenue) and which ones need a grain of salt (open rate, especially on Apple-heavy lists).
