A typo makes it to the final review, five minutes before a campaign sends. Not every fix at that point is actually safe.

Subject line, preview text, send time: safe to change right up until the campaign enters its sending queue. Klaviyo re-renders these at send time regardless of when they were last touched.

Segment and personalization: riskier. If content blocks were built against the original audience, swapping the segment afterward can leave personalization pulling data for the wrong group, producing blank or broken fields for some recipients.

And canceling isn't instant either. A cancellation triggered too close to send can leave part of the list with a version already delivered while the rest waits on the cancellation to finish, which is exactly how a duplicate send happens.

Five minutes before send is not the moment to improvise this decision. Worth having the checklist before the pressure hits, not during it.
