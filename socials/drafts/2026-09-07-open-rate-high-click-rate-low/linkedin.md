Your open rate says the subject line worked. Your click rate says the email didn't. That gap isn't mysterious, it's diagnosable.

We keep seeing the same pattern: 60% opens, under 0.5% clicks. That's not a deliverability problem. Deliverability got the email opened. What's breaking is one of three things, and it's rarely more than one at a time.

1. The subject line promised something specific, and the first line of the body didn't pay it off.
2. The email asked for too many actions. More links doesn't mean more chances to click, it means the reader has to decide which one matters.
3. The offer wasn't relevant to why that subscriber opened in the first place. That's a segment problem, not a copy problem.

Fix the biggest gap first, then re-measure before touching the others.
