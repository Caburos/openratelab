Everyone's asking whether Klaviyo's AI can just clean your list for you. Short answer: it scores, you decide.

Three AI features are genuinely live in Klaviyo today: predictive send time (timed per subscriber, based on their own history), AI-assisted subject line suggestions, and engagement scoring that feeds list health signals.

All three produce an output. None of them execute a strategy.

The engagement scoring flags who looks disengaged. It doesn't set your suppression window, decide how many re-engagement touches to send, or handle the subscriber who never responds. Those are still policy decisions someone has to make.

Where it genuinely helps: narrow, well-defined problems like send timing. Where it doesn't: flow architecture, segmentation tied to actual customer behavior, and brand voice in copy. Those need context Klaviyo's AI doesn't have.

Treat the scoring as an input to a list hygiene policy you've actually designed, not a replacement for having one.
