Switching to a new sending domain doesn't carry your old domain's reputation with it. Every migration starts from zero, no matter how established the brand behind it is.

The domain change itself isn't the risk. Sending full volume on day one is. A brand-new domain suddenly sending thousands of emails looks identical to inbox providers as a spike from any unknown sender, because from their side, that's exactly what it is.

Before the first send: SPF, DKIM, and DMARC configured and verified on the new domain directly, not copied blind from the old one. Then start with your smallest, most engaged segment, and widen volume and audience gradually over 2 to 4 weeks based on how the domain is actually performing.

Keep the old domain running in parallel the whole time. Only retire it once the new one has a genuinely proven track record.
