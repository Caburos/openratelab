A campaign already sent and the early numbers look bad. Most advice is about getting it right beforehand. Here's what's actually still fixable in the first hour or two, and what isn't.

Still fixable: a follow-up send to non-openers with a genuinely different subject line, the single highest-leverage move available. A broken link or wrong offer, correctable before more people click through to it. A related automation about to pile onto the same segment, still pausable.

Not fixable: the original subject line's open rate, locked in the moment it sent. Engagement fatigue from that specific send. Anyone who unsubscribed because of it.

Check opens and clicks in the first one to two hours, not the full 24 to 48 hour window. Most opens happen early. If something's mechanically wrong (broken link, bad offer), fix it now. If the numbers are just generically soft, that's a lesson for the next send, not this one.
