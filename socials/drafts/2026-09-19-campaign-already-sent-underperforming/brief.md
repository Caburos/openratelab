## Outline
- Why this moment gets skipped in most email advice, which focuses on pre-send optimization
- What's actually still fixable after a send: a follow-up send to non-openers with a different subject line, pausing a related automation that would compound the problem, correcting a broken link or offer before more people click it
- What's not fixable after the fact: the original subject line's performance, list fatigue from that specific send, anyone who already unsubscribed because of it
- How to tell which situation you're in fast, in the first hour or two, before the send's data is fully settled
- Turning it into a real lesson for the next send instead of just moving on
