A DTC brand we started working with had exactly $0 in email-attributed revenue the month before. Not underperforming. Nonexistent, confirmed on their own Klaviyo dashboard.

One month after launch: 50.62% of their total store revenue came from email.

The first campaign we sent, the very first email this list had ever received, generated 22% of that period's revenue on its own.

There was no email program before this. We built list capture, core flows, and a first campaign, in that order, then let the results speak.

The second month held at 33.70% of revenue, even as total store revenue dropped for a seasonal reason unrelated to email. The brand didn't need more traffic. It needed a system that was actually running.
