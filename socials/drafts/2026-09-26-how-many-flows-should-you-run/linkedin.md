"How many flows should I be running?" is the wrong first question. A brand with 12 active flows and real gaps in core coverage is worse off than one running 6 that actually map to the customer journey.

Most DTC brands need roughly 5 to 7 flows: welcome, abandoned cart, browse abandonment, post-purchase, win-back. That's the baseline that covers the moments driving the bulk of automated revenue.

Where accounts over-build: multiple near-duplicate win-back variants, or hyper-segmented versions that split an already-small audience too thin to actually perform.

Where accounts under-build: browse abandonment and post-purchase are the two flows most often missing entirely, both because they don't feel as urgent as an abandoned cart.

The audit that actually matters: list every active flow next to the customer moment it covers. What's duplicated, what's missing, what's actually doing its job.
