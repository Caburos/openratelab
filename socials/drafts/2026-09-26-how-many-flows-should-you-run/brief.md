## Outline

- Why "how many flows" is the wrong first question: a brand with 12 flows
  and gaps in the core lifecycle moments is worse off than one with 6
  flows that actually cover the customer journey
- The core coverage checklist: welcome, abandoned cart, browse
  abandonment, post-purchase, win-back/sunset, roughly 5-7 flows that
  cover the moments that actually drive revenue for most DTC brands
- Where accounts commonly over-build: multiple near-duplicate win-back
  variants, hyper-segmented versions of the same flow that split an
  already-small audience too thin
- Where accounts commonly under-build: skipping browse abandonment or
  post-purchase entirely, the two most commonly missing high-leverage
  flows
- A simple audit: list every active flow, map each one to the customer
  moment it covers, and see what's missing versus what's duplicated

## LinkedIn angle

"How many flows should I be running?" is the wrong question. Coverage of the moments that actually drive revenue matters more than the count.
