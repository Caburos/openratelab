A founder asked if email marketing would hurt their premium brand's image. Wrong question. The real question is whether the program is designed like a premium brand's, or like a discount blast list wearing one.

Email doesn't carry a reputation risk on its own. Three things do: sending the same generic, discount-led message to everyone regardless of engagement, copy and design that don't match the rest of the brand, and leading every send with price.

None of that requires sending less. It requires sending differently.

Segment by engagement and purchase behavior. Keep tone and design consistent with the brand, not a generic template. Mix value-first content with commercial sends so the inbox relationship isn't only about discounts.

A well-designed premium program can send as often as a discount brand and read completely differently, because the substance of each send is different, not just the frequency.

Cutting frequency to protect brand image usually just cuts revenue from a channel that was never the actual risk.
