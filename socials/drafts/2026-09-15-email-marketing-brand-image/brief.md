**Topic:** Does email marketing hurt a premium or high-end brand's image?
**Angle:** The risk isn't email marketing itself, it's frequency and tone run on autopilot. A premium brand can email often and still protect its positioning if the program is designed for it — this is a design problem, not a channel problem.

## Outline
- Why brands ask this question (email marketing's mass-market/discount reputation vs. premium positioning)
- The real risk factors: frequency without a plan, generic discount-first copy, sending everyone the same thing regardless of engagement or purchase history
- What protects brand image at high email frequency: segmentation by engagement and value tier, tone and design consistency with the brand (not just the storefront), value-first content mixed with commercial sends
- A premium brand's email program should look different from a discount brand's, not just send less
- Straight answer: no, if the program is designed for it; yes, if it's run like a discount blast list
