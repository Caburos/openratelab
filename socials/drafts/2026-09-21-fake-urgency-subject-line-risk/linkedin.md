"Last chance" subject lines are getting real attention right now. Not because urgency stopped working, because too many of the deadlines behind them were never real.

The risk isn't the phrase. It's the pattern: a "sale ends tonight" email followed by the same sale still running a week later. One exaggerated claim is a mistake. A repeated pattern is something a subscriber, or a regulator, can point to.

Flows are the highest-risk place to check first. A win-back sequence that reuses "last chance to stay on our list" on a fixed schedule, regardless of what's actually true for that subscriber, isn't a copy problem. It repeats for every person who hits that step.

The fix isn't softer language. It's making sure the deadline in the subject line actually matches what's configured on the platform: a real expiry, a real stock count, a real cutoff that's enforced, not just claimed.

Worth an audit of your last few months of urgency sends before this gets flagged from outside instead.
