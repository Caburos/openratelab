"Traffic but no conversion" gets diagnosed as an ads problem or a landing page problem almost every time. Before either, check whether you're even trying to bring people back. Most stores aren't.

Three flows catch traffic you already have: welcome, abandoned cart, and browse abandonment. Each one catches a visitor at a different point. If any one is missing, weak, or slow to fire, that's a specific leak, not a vague conversion problem.

A landing page fix only helps the visitors still on the page when you make it. A missing flow means every visitor who leaves without converting also leaves without a follow-up, which is a much bigger group.

Check flows first. It's the cheapest thing to rule out, and it's often the actual leak: welcome firing within minutes, abandoned cart within about an hour, some browse abandonment capture at all.

Fix that before spending more to bring in traffic that leaks the same way.
