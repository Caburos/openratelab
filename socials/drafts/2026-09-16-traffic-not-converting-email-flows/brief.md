# Brief — 2026-09-16 — traffic-not-converting-email-flows

**Mode:** blog-trend
**Topic:** Your store gets traffic but doesn't convert. Check your email flows before anything else.
**Angle:** Most "traffic but no conversion" diagnostics jump straight to ads, landing pages, or pricing. The cheapest, fastest check is whether the flows already built (welcome, abandoned cart, browse abandonment) are even capturing the traffic that's already showing up, before spending more to bring in traffic that leaks the same way.
**Target keyword:** shopify traffic not converting email marketing

## Outline
- Why "traffic but no conversion" usually gets diagnosed as an ads/landing-page problem first, and why that's often the wrong starting point
- The three flows that catch traffic already on-site or already engaged: welcome series, abandoned cart, browse abandonment. If these are missing or weak, paid traffic leaks before it ever gets a fair shot at converting
- How to check fast: is a welcome flow live and does it fire within minutes, does an abandoned cart flow actually exist and start within an hour, is there any browse-abandonment capture at all
- Why this check comes before CRO/landing-page work, not after: fixing a landing page doesn't help if the traffic it doesn't convert on-page was never going to get a second touch anyway
- What "good enough" looks like at a baseline level before moving on to deeper optimization

## LinkedIn angle
"Traffic but no conversion" gets diagnosed as an ads problem or a landing page problem almost every time. Before either, check whether you're even trying to bring people back. Most stores aren't.
