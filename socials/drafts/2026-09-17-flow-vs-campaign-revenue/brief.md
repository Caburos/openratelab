# Brief — 2026-09-17 — flow-vs-campaign-revenue

**Mode:** blog-trend
**Topic:** Why you should track flow revenue and campaign revenue separately, not blended
**Angle:** Klaviyo's default dashboard view makes it easy to see total attributed revenue without ever splitting flow versus campaign contribution.
