Most Klaviyo dashboards blend flow and campaign revenue into one number. That blend is hiding the actual signal. Split them, and you'll know in five minutes whether your automation or your calendar is doing the work.

Two accounts can show the same total attributed revenue and be completely different underneath. One carried almost entirely by flows. The other dependent on someone manually scheduling and sending every week.

Klaviyo's Analytics section lets you filter attributed revenue by flow versus by campaign instead of relying on the blended overview number. Takes a few minutes, no new tooling.

Flow-heavy split: generally healthy, automation is compounding whether or not anyone touches the account that week.

Campaign-heavy split: flows are likely undersized or missing key stages, and revenue depends on someone actively sending, not on the system working in the background.

Neither extreme is actually healthy long-term. The split just tells you where the next hour of work pays off.
