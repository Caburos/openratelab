01. ANALYSIS

This multi-species animal wellness brand had a healthy list and a real budget behind it. Most of its email revenue came from discount-driven promos.

That pattern trains subscribers to wait for the next sale instead of buying the moment an email lands. Every campaign was competing against the memory of the last discount.

PROGRAM LENGTH

8 MO

TYPICAL MONTHLY REVENUE

$90K–$150K

[ INFRASTRUCTURE ]

WHAT WE DID

We wanted to prove the list would respond to something other than a percentage off. So we built a send with no discount code attached at all.

01 / APPROACH

STORY-LED
CAMPAIGNS

We wrote a single campaign built entirely around brand narrative. No discount code, no urgency gimmick. The send leaned on a mission-linked angle the brand actually cared about, framed as a reason to buy today instead of waiting for a deal.

02 / PRINCIPLE

NARRATIVE
OVER DISCOUNTING

A STORY, NOT A DISCOUNT CODE, DROVE $35,972 IN ONE SEND.
