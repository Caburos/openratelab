01. ANALYSIS

This DTC pet wellness brand had years of send history but no consistent formula for what actually got opened. Open rates swung depending on who wrote the subject line that week.

Some sends did well. Others barely registered. There was no repeatable system behind the good weeks, just guesswork that occasionally landed.

[ INFRASTRUCTURE ]

WHAT WE DID

We stopped guessing and started testing one variable at a time, until a clear, repeatable pattern showed up in the data.

01 / APPROACH

CURIOSITY-DRIVEN
SUBJECT LINES

We standardized on curiosity-driven, non-promotional subject lines instead of generic sale language. We paired them with holiday-tied campaigns offering a consistent incentive, tested and repeated across more than four separate occasions.
