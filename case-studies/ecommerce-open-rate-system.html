FREQUENTLY ASKED

How did you achieve a 75.2% open rate? [+]

The result came from three things working together: a full flow architecture rebuild that removed duplicate and competing sends, a subject line approach built on curiosity and specificity rather than offers, and a list health overhaul that stopped sending to chronically unengaged contacts. No single change produced 75.2%. It was the system working as a whole.

Can results like this be replicated for other brands? [+]

The specific number varies by brand, list size, and category, but the methodology is repeatable. Every account we've rebuilt from the same starting point (broken flow architecture, weak subject lines, unclean list) has seen significant open rate improvement. The audit tells us what's pulling your numbers down. That's where we start.
